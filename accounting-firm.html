What is the difference between bookkeeping and tax planning?

Bookkeeping keeps financial records current, while tax planning helps reduce surprises by shaping decisions before filing deadlines arrive.

When should a business hire an accountant?

Businesses often benefit once revenue grows, payroll begins, or owners need better visibility into cash flow and profitability.

Do you work with monthly retainers?

Yes. Recurring accounting retainers are common for bookkeeping, advisory meetings, and annual tax coordination.
